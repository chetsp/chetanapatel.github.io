# Logo sources

Vector wordmarks from the VectorLogoZone catalogue:

- Tesla: https://github.com/VectorLogoZone/vectorlogozone/blob/main/src/content/logos/tesla/tesla-ar21.svg
- Bank of America: https://github.com/VectorLogoZone/vectorlogozone/blob/main/src/content/logos/bankofamerica/bankofamerica-ar21.svg
- George Mason University: https://github.com/VectorLogoZone/vectorlogozone/blob/main/src/content/logos/gmu_edu/gmu_edu-ar21.svg

The SVG viewBoxes are cropped to remove surrounding whitespace. The original vector lettering, shapes, and colors are retained. Assets are served locally on a white surface in both themes for legibility; no third-party image requests or brand-font downloads are needed.
